ABOUT US

OUR STORY

With a legacy spanning more than three decades, Classy Captures has been crafting timeless wedding stories filled with love, emotion, and authenticity.

We’re more than photographers, we’re storytellers who turn weddings into timeless works of art.

As one of the top wedding photographers in South India, we blend emotion and innovation to tell love stories that stand the test of time.

Under the leadership of Pavan Sharma, our second-generation Creative Director, we continue to push the boundaries of luxury wedding photography in Bangalore. His eye for detail and love for storytelling have guided Classy Captures to become a name couples trust with their most precious

memories.

BEYONG WEDDINGS

Our craft extends beyond weddings into the Kannada Film Industry, where our expertise in cinematography and aerial filming has earned recognition and respect.

From intimate weddings to large-scale film productions, our visual storytelling has touched countless hearts and screens.
